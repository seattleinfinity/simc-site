# Resources

- [Art of Problem Solving](https://artofproblemsolving.com/): Online classes and problem-solving resources.
- [X-Camp](https://www.x-camp.org/): Coding and mathematics programs for students.
- [3Blue1Brown](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCYO_jab_esuFRV4b17AJtAw): Visual explanations of mathematics.
- [Numberphile](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCoxcjq-8xIDTYp3uz647V5A): Conversations about numbers, puzzles, and mathematical ideas.
- [AoPS Bookstore](https://artofproblemsolving.com/store): Books and problem-solving resources.
- [Things to Make and Do in the Fourth Dimension](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/23008136-things-to-make-and-do-in-the-fourth-dimension): Matt Parker’s playful tour of mathematical ideas.
- [Real Mathematical Analysis](https://smartmanmaths.files.wordpress.com/2017/11/real-mathematical-analysis.pdf): Charles Pugh’s analysis textbook.
- [A Mathematician’s Lament](https://www.maa.org/external_archive/devlin/LockhartsLament.pdf): An essay about mathematics education.
