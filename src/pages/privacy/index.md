## Mailing list

When you join the SIMC mailing list, we collect your email address and signup time to manage your subscription. To request removal, email [seattleinfinitymathcircle@gmail.com](mailto:seattleinfinitymathcircle@gmail.com?subject=Unsubscribe).

## Bot protection

The signup form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help prevent automated submissions. Cloudflare processes information to provide this check, as described in its [Turnstile Privacy Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).
